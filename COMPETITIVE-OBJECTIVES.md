# GoreeCloud Notes — Competitive Objectives

This record defines the products and capability areas GoreeCloud Notes uses as benchmarks for continuous improvement. Competitive targets are not current-feature claims.

## Primary benchmark products

- Evernote
- TriliumNext / Trilium Notes
- Joplin
- Obsidian
- Standard Notes
- Comparable private, self-hosted, portable, or knowledge-management note applications

## Capabilities to match or improve

- Fast note creation with a low-friction capture path
- Rich structured editing with reliable Markdown and machine-readable portability
- Notebooks, collections, tags, templates, and deeper organization
- Powerful indexed search and filtering
- Attachments and inline images
- Internal links, backlinks, connected-note navigation, and knowledge relationships
- Revision history, recovery, and safe deletion workflows
- Import from major note formats and migration sources
- Full-library export and re-import without vendor lock-in
- Responsive web/PWA use and accessible keyboard interaction
- Offline and future synchronization capabilities where they can be implemented safely
- Browser clipping and future native client workflows where separately approved
- Strong authentication, user isolation, quotas, auditability, and administrative controls

## Objectives GoreeCloud Notes should exceed

- Private, self-hosted operation without requiring a proprietary cloud account
- Open or documented export formats and verified round-trip portability
- Provider-neutral migration evidence instead of silent lossy conversion
- Strong attachment ownership, authorization, integrity, quota, backup, and restore controls
- Reproducible dependencies, deterministic migrations, and tested recovery
- First-party GoreeCloud architecture, data model, APIs, release process, and product direction
- Glaze UI consistency and accessibility
- Integration with GoreeCloud Identity, Notify, Browser, Memos, and other approved Suite products without surrendering Notes data authority
- No required external AI provider, advertising, sponsorship, or analytics dependency

## Capabilities intentionally rejected or bounded

- Public-by-default notes or social-discovery behavior as the normal product model
- Proprietary-cloud lock-in or export paths that cannot reconstruct the user's library
- Required OpenAI, Gemini, or other external AI APIs for core note functionality
- Treating GoreeCloud Memos as a disposable subset of Notes; Memos remains the separate quick-capture product
- Claiming migration success when content, metadata, attachments, ownership, searchability, and exportability have not been validated

## Competitive review rule

Competitor capabilities should be evaluated for usefulness, privacy, security, portability, accessibility, recoverability, and long-term ownership. GoreeCloud Notes should adopt or exceed ideas that materially improve the knowledge workspace without adding unnecessary complexity or weakening the application's private, self-hosted foundation.
